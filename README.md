# PPO-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning for Parcel Singulation

Deep Reinforcement Learning controller for automated parcel singulation on a **5×5 Actuated Matrix Sorter (AMS)**, developed in MATLAB using **Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)**.

The goal of the project is to transform a clustered flow of parcels into a properly spaced stream while preserving high throughput and limiting unnecessary actuation.

Developed as a final project for the **Machine Learning for Mechanical Systems** course at **Politecnico di Milano**.

---

## Overview

Parcel singulation is a key task in automated logistics: incoming parcels must be progressively separated before reaching downstream sorting and handling systems.

Instead of relying on a fixed rule-based controller, the problem is formulated as a **continuous-control Markov Decision Process (MDP)**.

A PPO Actor-Critic agent learns how to control each cell of a 5×5 Actuated Matrix Sorter by adjusting:

- parcel forwarding velocity;
- local actuation direction.

The learned policy aims to simultaneously:

- create sufficient longitudinal spacing between parcels;
- maintain forward flow and throughput;
- avoid unstable or unnecessarily aggressive actions.

## Reinforcement Learning Formulation

### Observation Space

The AMS is discretized into **25 sensing cells**.

For each cell, four normalized features are provided to the agent:

- occupancy;
- lateral position;
- longitudinal position;
- parcel size.

This results in a **100-dimensional observation vector**.

### Action Space

For each of the 25 AMS cells, the PPO policy controls:

- actuation angle;
- forwarding velocity.

The resulting action space is a **50-dimensional continuous vector**.

---

## PPO Architecture

The controller is based on a continuous-action **PPO Actor-Critic architecture**.

Both Actor and Critic use Multi-Layer Perceptrons with hidden layers:

```text
512 → 256 → 128
```

with ReLU activations.

The Actor generates continuous commands for the AMS cells, while the Critic estimates the state value used during policy optimization.

The final policy was trained for **25,000 episodes**.

---

## Reward Engineering

A major part of the project focused on iterative reward-function design.

The final reward combines four components:

```text
R_total = R_progress + R_gradient + R_exit + R_action
```

### Forward Progress

Encourages parcels to keep moving toward the output and prevents the policy from solving the spacing problem by simply stopping the flow.

### Singulation Gradient

Provides a continuous reward signal based on the longitudinal gap between consecutive parcels and their relative velocity.

### Exit Performance

Evaluates the final spacing when parcels cross the downstream evaluation line.

A target gap of:

```text
gap ≥ 0.15 m
```

is used as the main singulation objective.

### Action Regularization

Penalizes excessive velocity and rotation commands to discourage unnecessary actuation and oscillatory behavior.

## Results

Iterative reward engineering and PPO hyperparameter tuning significantly improved the policy performance.

| Configuration | Singulation Rate | Average Gap | Throughput |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Medium v1.0 | 48.9% | 0.428 m | 9,330 PPH |
| Medium v2.0 | 90.0% | 0.515 m | 8,924 PPH |
| **Final policy** | **100.0%** | **0.477 m** | **8,842 PPH** |

The final PPO policy achieved a **100% singulation rate under nominal test conditions** while maintaining a throughput of approximately **8,842 parcels/hour**.

---

## Robustness Testing

The final policy was also evaluated outside its nominal operating conditions to identify the physical and operational limits of the learned controller.

The stress-testing campaign included variations in:

- parcel generation frequency;
- total number of parcels;
- downstream extraction speed;
- number of parcels generated simultaneously.

The trained controller remained highly stable during long-duration tests, while extreme traffic density and insufficient downstream extraction speed revealed the physical limits of the simulated system.

## Running the Project

### Requirements

The project was developed in **MATLAB** and requires the **Reinforcement Learning Toolbox**.

### Training

The main training workflow is contained in:

```matlab
RL_main.m
```

The custom AMS environment and reward logic are implemented in:

```matlab
RL_environment.m
```

Training a PPO policy from scratch can require a significant amount of computation. The final **25,000-episode training run** was GPU-accelerated.

### Visualization

Simulation and policy behavior can be visualized using:

```matlab
AMSVisualizer_simple.m
```

The plotting scripts can be used to analyze saved training and evaluation data.
